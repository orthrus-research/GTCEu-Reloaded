## The GregTech name and assets

GTCEu is an indirect descendant of GregTech, created with GregoriusT's knowledge and permission.
The project's records of those permissions are kept in [docs/permission](docs/permission):

- [permission.png](docs/permission/permission.png): GregoriusT grants permission to use his
  textures in the rewrite.
- [permission2.png](docs/permission/permission2.png): the GregTech name may be used as long as
  "Community Edition" is part of it and it is clear that GregoriusT is not the one doing it.
- [permission3.png](docs/permission/permission3.png): GregoriusT confirms that he gave consent.
- [permission4.png](docs/permission/permission4.png): Archengius, author of GregTech Community
  Edition, allows anyone to distribute GTCE under any name, with any changes (June 17, 2018).
